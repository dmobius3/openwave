## 1. Equations first

### 1.1 The problem

On sections of the flat unitary bundle `E_σ` over `X = S³/Γ`, `σ ∈ {3′, 4}`, with `Γ` generated by the two unit quaternions of the handout ([`../m8_10/worklist_as_run.md`](../m8_10/worklist_as_run.md) § 1.2):

```text
(−Δ − λ) ψ + g N(ψ) = 0,        N(ψ) = |ψ|² ψ   (pointwise)
```

Level `n` carries `−Δ = n(n+2)`, the Haar measure has `∫_X 1 = 1`, and the block `K` is the seven-dimensional level-6 space. A fibre vector `v ∈ V_3` lifts to the block section `Φ = Φ_{σ,v}`, normalized so that `∫_X |Φ|² = 1`.

### 1.2 The expansion and the three equations the run computes

```text
ψ = aΦ + a³ξ + a⁵ζ + …,      λ = 48 + λ₂ a² + λ₄ a⁴ + …,      ξ, ζ ⊥ K

order a³, block:      λ₂ Φ = g Π_K N(Φ)            ⇒  λ₂ = g κ,  where Π_K N(Φ) = κ Φ
order a³, range:      A ξ = −g Π_⊥ N(Φ),   A = −Δ − 48
                      ‖Π_n ξ‖² = g² ‖Π_n N(Φ)‖² / (n(n+2) − 48)²
order a⁵, block:      λ₄ Φ = g Π_K DN_Φ[ξ]         (consistency: the part ⊥ Φ must vanish)
                      λ₄ = g ⟨Φ, DN_Φ[ξ]⟩ = −3 g² Σ_n ‖Π_n N(Φ)‖² / (n(n+2) − 48)
```

with `DN_Φ[h] = 2 Re(Φ†h) Φ + |Φ|² h`. The sum runs over the sector's levels above 6, and the cubic of a level-6 section has no content above level 18.

### 1.3 The block cubic, and the rays

```text
Π_6 N(Φ_{σ,v}) = Φ_{σ, w(v)},    w(v) = (d/7) ‖v‖² v − ((7−d)/√91) M₆(v),    d = dim σ
M_K(v) = [[v ⊗ Θv]_K ⊗ v]_3
```

`R1 = v_3`, `R2 = v_0`, `R3 = v_2 + v_{−2}`, `R4 = v_3 + v_{−3}` (coherent, zonal, octahedron, hexagon) and `R5 = cos t·v_2 + sin t·v_{−3}`, `sin²t = 12/25` (the pentagonal pyramid, the negative control).

### 5.3 The block cubic's form is not unique

The seven maps `M_K` span a four-dimensional space (rank 4, singular values 109, 40.7, 25.7, 23.7, then `1e-14`). The solver found this and gave the exact relation table; the auditor confirmed it and corrected its own stage-1 wording, which had called the vanishing `M_1` to `M_5` coefficients forced. A1's frozen form is one valid representation, and no value moves.

### 5.4 The arguments, compared

| Point | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Level 16 at `R2`, `R3`, `R4` | time reversal forces it: with `Θv = ±v`, the level-6 coupling is proportional to `f²` and the level-8 coupling to the Jacobian `(f², f)_1 = 0` (auditor). The solver's exact two-term cancellation at `R4` is rigorous; its "not forced by symmetry" holds only for the stabilizer |
| The sign of `λ₄` | both arguments are complete. The solver's needs only `κ > 1`, since `\|Φ\|²` has levels 0 and 12 only, so `κ = 1 + ‖Π₁₂\|Φ\|²‖²` |

| `R5` | parallelism at order `a³` is not forced by symmetry there and holds by exact evaluation; the order-`a⁵` orthogonal part lies along `e = sin t·v_2 − cos t·v_{−3}` with coefficient `−7188839√91/81061695000` in `3′` and `+19565553√273/384292480000` in `4`, opposite in sign |
