## 1. Equations first

### 1.1 The problem

On sections of the flat unitary bundle `E_σ` over `X = S³/Γ`, `σ ∈ {3′, 4}`, with `Γ` generated by the two unit quaternions of the handout ([`../m8_11/worklist.md`](../m8_11/worklist.md) § 1.2):

```text
F(ψ, λ) = (−Δ − λ) ψ + g N(ψ) = 0,        N(ψ) = |ψ|² ψ   (pointwise),   g real, g ≠ 0
```

Level `n` carries `−Δ = n(n+2)`, the Haar measure has `∫_X 1 = 1`, and the block `K` is the seven-dimensional level-6 space. A fibre vector `u ∈ V_3` lifts to the block section `Φ = Φ_{σ,u}`, normalized so that `∫_X |Φ|² = 1`. `F` is the `⟨·,·⟩_ℝ`-gradient of `E_λ(ψ) = ½⟨ψ, (−Δ − λ)ψ⟩ + (g/4)∫|ψ|⁴`, which is invariant under `G = SU(2)_left × U(1)`.

### 1.2 The expansion with a tilt

```text
ψ = aΦ + a³(κ + ξ) + a⁵ζ + …,      λ = 48 + λ₂ a² + λ₄ a⁴ + …,      κ ∈ K, ⟨Φ, κ⟩ = 0,  ξ, ζ ⊥ K

order a³, block:   Π_K N(Φ) = Q Φ,  λ₂ = g Q,  Q = ∫|Φ|⁴           (Φ critical for Q on ℙ(K))
order a³, range:   A ξ = −g Π_⊥ N(Φ),   A = −Δ − 48
order a⁵, block:   g Π_K DN_Φ[κ + ξ] − λ₂ κ − λ₄ Φ = 0,   DN_Φ[h] = 2 Re(Φ†h) Φ + |Φ|² h
   tangential:     L_T κ = −Π_T DN_Φ[ξ],   L_T = Π_T DN_Φ − Q  on the slice tangent T
   along Φ:        λ₄ = g ⟨Φ, DN_Φ[ξ]⟩ = −3 g² Σ_n ‖Π_n N(Φ)‖² / (n(n+2) − 48)
```

**The bridge.** For a unit tangent `E ⊥ Φ` in the block, `d²Q(cos s·Φ + sin s·E)/ds² = 4(⟨E, DN_Φ E⟩_ℝ − Q)` at `s = 0`, so `L_T = ¼ Hess_T Q`, and `Q = 1 + w₆(σ) r̂₆` gives `L_T = (w₆/4) Hess_T r̂₆`, with `w₆(3′) = 28/39` and `w₆(4) = 21/52`. One set of numbers decides both the nondegeneracy of the reduced problem and the tilt.

### 1.3 The six rays and the two new tangents

`U1 = v_3`, `U2 = v_0`, `U3 = v_2 + v_{−2}`, `U4 = v_3 + v_{−3}` (the coherent, zonal, octahedron and hexagon rays), `U5 = cos t·v_2 + sin t·v_{−3}` with `sin²t = 12/25` (the pentagonal pyramid) and `U6 = v_3 + z₀ v_0 + v_{−3}` with `z₀ = √(23/10)` (the trigonal prism). At U5 the tangent `e_t` is the unit section of `−sin t·v_2 + cos t·v_{−3}`, and `i·e_t` is a rotation orbit. At U6, `τ_x` is the unit section of the part of `v_0` orthogonal to U6, and `τ_y = i·τ_x`.

### 1.4 The existence theorem, in one line

Blow up (`ψ = a(Φ + k) + a³w`, `λ = 48 + a²μ`), divide by `a³` to remove the trivial branch `ψ = 0`, solve the range equation by the implicit function theorem on `K^⊥`, and solve the reduced block equation on a slice transverse to the `G`-orbit, where `L_T` is invertible. The theorem as the author stated it (T.a to T.e) is transcribed in the stage-2 packet [`../m8_11/theorem_stage2.md`](../m8_11/theorem_stage2.md) and graded in § 5.
