The sweep excludes the 192 of its 20001 points lying within 0.01 rad of an axis, the exclusion the note's own "What the check script does" section states, so it bounds the maximum away from the three equality directions and says nothing inside those neighborhoods.

So the note's argument was never checked end to end by that script, and its equality set in particular rested on the note's own tracing, corroborated by a sweep that skips the neighborhoods where equality holds.
