# M8.11 worklist dry run: plan and frozen criteria

Written 2026-09-13 and frozen before launch; its SHA-256 goes in the launch log. It governs one run, by a fresh author-side context, of `worklist.md` as the only packet file.

## Purpose

Instrument adequacy only. The run asks whether the worklist reaches every claim, poses nothing underdetermined, leaks nothing, and draws gradable arguments where a claim is an argument. Numerical agreement is secondary. Repairs afterwards touch the instrument only; no frozen value moves.

## Criterion 1: item 10 (T1)

The existence argument is scored against seven elements:
1. the function spaces, and why the nonlinearity is smooth on them;
2. the Fredholm structure, and the inverse on the complement of the kernel;
3. the fixed space and its invariance;
4. the reduction, with its gradient structure;
5. the nondegeneracy hypothesis;
6. where the trivial branch is divided out, or an equivalent treatment of the degeneracy at `a = 0`;
7. the identification of Taylor coefficients with the formal ones.

The uniqueness statement (its neighborhoods and symmetry quotient) and the regularity claimed are also recorded.

Outcomes, fixed now:
- **Gradable:** at least 5 of the 7, with element 6 present.
- **Element 6 missing:** fail, not partial, whatever the count.
- **4 of 7, with element 6:** item 10's wording is sharpened, without naming the method, and T1's bar stands.
- **3 or fewer:** T1's bar is rewritten before filing.

## Criterion 2: U6's zeros (S1)

Recorded: whether item 9 gives a common structural reason for U6's zeros (the tilt and the forcing along `τ_y`, and the cross term), such as an antiunitary symmetry, or only exact cancellations.
- **Structural reason found:** S1 is reachable blind.
- **Cancellations only:** the final instrument may sharpen its structural prompt, without defining `S`.

An S1 failure in the real run is a pre-registered legitimate outcome either way.

## Criterion 3: the rest

- **Coverage:** every claim's quantities come back (A1, A2, T2, T3, T4, B1, C1, C2, D1 to D4, N1, X0, X4).
- **Underdetermined questions:** each question the agent reports as underdetermined is an instrument defect to repair.
- **Normalization:** the section-normalized convention holds; a fibre-normalized value is an instrument defect if the worklist allowed it.
- **Values:** a mechanical comparison against the frozen values, secondary to the above.

## Mechanics, as M8.10's dry run

- **The room:** a fresh headless session in a room outside every repository, holding only `worklist.md` and a short `BRIEF.md`.
- **Containment:** web tools are disabled and reads of the answer locations are denied. A canary run first shows that nothing answer-bearing loads at startup.
- **The audit:** the transcript is audited for any tool call outside the room, with the audit pattern mutation-tested before use.
- **What gets kept:** the brief and the return join the author's package after the privacy scan. The raw transcript stays author-side unless it passes that scan unredacted, and its audit result is reported either way.
