**Status**: 🚧 PLANNED STUB, CONDITIONAL. ## Scope: orthodoxy first

The challenge (Fleury, 2026-08-02, PS2): "Recreating the stable knots we already know in something like OpenWave would be a great starting point for a systematic exploration of stable configurations and their excitation spectra. Can OpenWave find the known knots?"

| Piece | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Seed machinery (the feasibility gate) | Non-axisymmetric closed-curve defect seeding from a parametric knot curve (the current seeders are cylindrically symmetric); trefoil 3_1 and figure-eight 4_1 first, the Tait table as the target list |
| Reads | Per-knot: does the relaxed state retain the knot type (or unknot/escape); energy ordering vs crossing number vs the Tait table; charge class per the census instrument |
| Stretch | Excitation spectra of surviving knots (the challenge's second half); the knot-table figure against the author's ring-count frame |
| Boundary | 3×3 only, toy parameters, current hardware; anything beyond folds into [M5.22.9](m5_22_9_task_details.md) |

## Definition of done

The trefoil + figure-eight verdicts measured (survival, energy, charge) with the seeding machinery documented, or the feasibility spike's honest negative; full closeout per the series policy.

## Gated by

User pick + the seed-machinery feasibility spike. Non-gating for the series mainline (the beta-decay and dynamical rungs do not depend on it).
